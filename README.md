## 🙋‍♀️ About Me

**Role:** AWS Alliance Manager  
**Start Date:** June 8, 2026  
**Location:** Austin, Texas

My husband and I have two kids who are now 19 and 22. My path here has been a little unconventional. For many years, my focus was on raising my family. We lived in eight states in ten years, and for part of that adventure I homeschooled my kids. Along the way, I worked in education and spent many years coaching softball, which gave me a love for helping people grow, build confidence, and achieve goals they didn't think were possible.

In the last ten years, I found myself gravitating toward operations and eventually into technology, where I've spent the last several years. I've held a variety of roles throughout my career, and each one has given me a greater appreciation for the challenges, priorities, and perspectives that different teams bring to the table. It's helped me understand that everyone is solving a different piece of the puzzle, and I genuinely enjoy figuring out how those pieces fit together.

Outside of work, I'm passionate about health and wellness, a huge sports fan, and always have a podcast playing somewhere in the background. I love concerts, live music, and hockey. I'm also slightly obsessed with coffee and firmly believe some of the best conversations, ideas, and friendships start over a good cup of it.
