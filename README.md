# Krystal Hamilton

## 🙋‍♀️ About Me

**Role:** Strategic Partnership and Alliance Lead
**Start Date:** June 8, 2026
**Location:** Austin, Texas

My husband and I have two kids who are now 19 and 22. My path here has been a little unconventional. For many years, my focus was on raising my family. We lived in eight states in ten years, and for part of that adventure I homeschooled my kids. Along the way, I worked in education and spent many years coaching softball, which gave me a love for helping people grow, build confidence, and achieve goals they didn't think were possible.

In the last ten years, I found myself gravitating toward operations and eventually into technology. I've held a variety of roles throughout my career, and each one has given me a greater appreciation for the challenges, priorities, and perspectives that different teams bring to the table. It's helped me understand that everyone is solving a different piece of the puzzle, and I genuinely enjoy figuring out how those pieces fit together.

Outside of work, I'm passionate about health and wellness, a huge sports fan, and always have a podcast playing somewhere in the background. I love concerts, live music, and hockey. I'm also slightly obsessed with coffee and firmly believe some of the best conversations, ideas, and friendships start over a good cup of it.

## 🚀 Focus Areas

As Strategic Partnership and Alliance Lead, I manage and grow Liatrio's key technology alliances — including **AWS**, **Anthropic**, **OpenAI** — alongside other strategic technology partnerships across the organization.

My focus is on identifying growth opportunities, building strong partner relationships, creating alignment between alliance activity and Liatrio's broader business goals, and ensuring our partnerships drive meaningful outcomes for both our clients and our teams.
